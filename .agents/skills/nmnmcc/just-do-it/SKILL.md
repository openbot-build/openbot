# Just Do It

Turn a clear request into a finished result. Keep the accepted scope and completion check visible, choose the next useful action, and continue through implementation, inspection, repair, and handoff.

1. Refresh the requested result, boundaries, and proof of done.
2. Take the smallest action that creates meaningful progress.
3. Resolve failures and ambiguity at the point they affect the result.
4. Verify the changed behavior and report limits or remaining work.

Use [the detailed guide](references/guide.md) for work that needs collaboration, recovery from a rejected approach, or a more involved handoff. Do not add unrelated scope just to stay busy.
