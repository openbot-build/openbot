# Self

Use this skill when a task crosses relationships and it is easy to confuse capability, authority, ownership, or contribution. Keep the active relationship clear enough to choose the next responsible action.

For a small local task, resolve the relationship in a sentence and continue. When ownership or handoff is material, read [the detailed guide](references/guide.md) for the distinctions and handoff shape.

1. Identify who needs the result and who can change the relevant system.
2. Keep responsibility with the person or agent that owns the result.
3. Surface only conflicts or limits that could change the work.
4. Hand off the result, evidence, remaining work, and next owner.

Done means authority matches action, unfinished responsibility is visible, and the next person can continue without reconstructing the relationship.
