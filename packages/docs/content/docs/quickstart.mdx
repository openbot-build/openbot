Use this guide on an OpenBot service that your organization already runs. You
need its client or a linked [Channel](/docs/channels), an active membership, and
a Bot with a working model.

## Start a conversation

1. [Sign in](/docs/authentication) and choose the organization where you want to
   work.
2. Choose a Bot you may use. Ask your administrator for one if none is
   available.
3. Send a small task: “Make a list of three things to check before a meeting.”
4. Read the answer in the same conversation. You can send a correction or a
   further question there.

OpenBot keeps one direct Chat between you and that Bot. Your client may show the
answer as it arrives or when it is complete.

## Give the Bot more work

State the result you want and give the needed facts. Add [files](/docs/files) or
connect an [App](/docs/apps) when the task needs them. Long work can come back
later through [delegation](/docs/delegation).

If a [request](/docs/requests) arrives, check the action and its scope before
you decide. An answer in the Chat does not always mean all background work has
ended.

## If you cannot start

A missing organization needs an invitation or access from its administrator. A
Bot without an available model needs a model choice. Ask your administrator when
you cannot choose one. For a missing operation in your client, ask the client
provider.
