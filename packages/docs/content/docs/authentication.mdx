See [account access](/access) for invitation-only upgrade defaults, email login,
approvals, group roles and admin recovery.

Use the client address your organization gave you. OpenBot uses an outside
sign-in provider; it does not keep your password.

## Sign in

Start sign-in in your client. Give your work email when asked, so OpenBot can
use the right provider. Complete sign-in at that provider, then return to the
client. If no provider is available, contact your administrator.

## Choose an organization

Choose the organization for this work when you belong to more than one. Signing
in alone does not make you a member. Accept an invitation, or join through the
domain or sign-in service your administrator has set up. A suspended membership
cannot do work.

Your direct Chats and App connections belong to your space in that organization.
Switching organizations does not move them.

## Manage access

A user token is a secret that lets a program act for you in one organization.
Create one only when a tool you use needs it. Give it a clear name and an end
date. Save the secret when it is first shown; you cannot read it again. Revoke
it when the tool no longer needs access. A token cannot do more than your
account may do.

Sign out when you finish on a shared device. For password or sign-in recovery,
use your sign-in provider. For membership and rights, contact your organization
administrator.
