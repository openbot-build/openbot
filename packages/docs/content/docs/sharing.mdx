description: "Share Bots"
---

Share a Bot when another person should use its role and instructions in their
own organization. You need the right to share that Bot.

## Invite a person

Use your client to invite a person by email or make an invitation link. Only an
active top Bot can be shared: one owned by a person or organization. An email
invitation lasts seven days. A link can serve more than one person.

## Accept and work

Sign in, accept the invitation, and choose your organization. You get a linked
Bot there. Its name, role, instructions, and model come from the source Bot. It
works with your access and App connections. The source owner does not gain
access to your Chats. Your organization bears the model usage.

## End access

Revoking an invitation stops new acceptances; it does not remove people who
already joined. Remove a Bot member to end their access. Archiving the source
Bot also stops linked Bots. As a recipient, archive your linked Bot to leave.
