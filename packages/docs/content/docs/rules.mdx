A Grant keeps an allow or deny decision for a Bot capability. A Rule states in
plain language when a Bot may do work. Both can affect later
[requests](/docs/requests).

## Keep a decision

When answering a request, choose the scope you intend. A Chat Grant applies in
that Chat; a lifetime Grant stays until revoked. Write a Rule only when you want
a condition judged on later work. State the action, target, and condition
clearly.

## Review saved choices

Use your client to read the Rules and Grants you may manage. Revoke a Grant when
the permission is no longer wanted. Change or delete a Rule when the task
changes. These choices do not grant rights beyond your organization access.

## When a request still arrives

A Rule needs a judge model set by your administrator. If the judge cannot
decide, a Bot or person must decide. A saved choice also may not match the new
action or target. Read the new request before changing a wider rule.
