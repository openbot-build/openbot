---
title: "Let a Bot use secrets"
description: "Let a Bot use secrets"
---

A Vault holds items that let a Bot use secrets without reading their values. A
secret can be stored in OpenBot or read from a secret provider.

## Prepare an item

Use the Vault functions in your client to choose or create a Vault you may
manage. Add an item with a clear name and the secret fields it needs. Set its
exits: the App tools and arguments that may receive the secret. Ask your
administrator when an outside secret provider must be set up.

## Give a Bot access

Grant the Bot access to the Vault only after checking its items and exits. This
gives access to every item in that Vault, within each item’s exits. In
[Code Mode](/docs/code-mode), the Bot uses a reference. OpenBot puts the secret
into an allowed App call. A call with a secret reference needs a decision.

## End access

Remove the Bot access when the task ends. Deleting a Vault deletes its items and
Bot access, but does not delete secrets from outside providers. If a call is
denied, check both the Bot access and the item’s allowed exits.
