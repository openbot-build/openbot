A Bot is a lasting worker with a name, a role, instructions, and
[memory](/memory). Use your client to choose a Bot that you own or may use.

## Make a Bot

Give the Bot a name, state its job, and write the instructions it should follow.
Choose an available language model. A production Bot without a model cannot
answer. If you cannot make a Bot or choose a model, ask your administrator for
access.

You can choose a default Bot for clients that use it. A [trigger](/triggers)
with no named Bot also uses it. A new account has no default Bot.

## Work with a Bot

Send the Bot a task in your [Chat](/chats). It can give long work to itself or
another Bot through [delegation](/delegation). New actions may need your
[decision](/requests). A shared Bot uses the access and App connections of the
person it works for.

## Change or archive a Bot

Change its name, role, instructions, or model when you have the right. Archiving
also archives the Bots it owns. Restoring it restores only that Bot. If it was
your default Bot, choose another before using triggers that need a default.
Sharing and linked Bots have further rules in [Share Bots](/sharing).
