An App is an outside service your Bot can use. Connect it through your client
with your own account.

## Connect an App

Find an App in the [App Index](/docs/app-index) or give its HTTPS address.
Review the name, source, endpoint, and required access. When the address lists
several Apps, choose one. Finding an App does not connect it.

Confirm the connection. If the App needs sign-in, sign in at its provider and
return to your client. Check that the connection is ready before asking the Bot
to use it.

## Use the connection

Tell the Bot what you want to do. A call can need a [decision](/docs/requests)
or more information from you. Read the result in the Chat. Some clients also
show an App view; a text result is available too.

If an operation has an unknown outcome, check the actual state in the App before
repeating it. It may have done the work already.

## Maintain your Apps

Sign in again when access expires. Review a connection again when the App
changes where it sends requests or what access it needs. Remove connections you
no longer use. An exported App list contains no secrets or sign-ins. Importing
it makes drafts that still need review and connection.

Removing a connection can take time while OpenBot closes active work. Local
removal can finish before the provider revokes its access. Remote cleanup may
wait while another connection still uses related access. Check the cleanup state
in your client.

An upgrade keeps saved App history. Some old work cannot continue without its
original caller. Check an unknown result in the App before starting new work.
