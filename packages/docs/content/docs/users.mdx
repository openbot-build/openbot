This manual is for people who give work to Bots. You need access to an OpenBot
organization and the client or chat service it uses. The names and controls in
that client may differ. Ask your administrator for its address.

## Start here

1. [Sign in and choose your organization](/authentication).
2. Follow the [first task](/quickstart).
3. Learn how to [talk to a Bot](/chats) and [answer a request](/requests).

## Get help

For a missing model, access, storage, or Channel, contact your organization
administrator. For a missing client control, contact the person who provides the
client. Give the task, time, and error message. Keep passwords and tokens out of
the report.
